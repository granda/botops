# Hard rules and quiet default

## Quiet by default

- CoS is quiet when there is nothing for Matthew to decide.
- L2 does not talk to Matthew.
- Swarm noise stays in the swarm.
- Cadence fires with zero findings stay quiet toward Matthew.
- CoS is the sole interface. If he wants to talk to an L2 or L3, that is a choice, not the design.

The failure mode this prevents: a useful bot that texts him every time it breathes.

## Rules that do not bend

- Only L3 launches Cursor cloud agents and owns recurring routines.
- Multi-L3 jobs always swarm, with claim and dedupe. Max six in a channel.
- No new durable L2 until Matthew says yes.
- CoS chat stays decision-dense. No browse logs, no “here’s what I clicked,” no play-by-play. Intake, assignment, ticket link.

What he actually does, most days: talk to CoS, open the tickets it surfaces, and sweep 🗑 / 💀 names. The fleet does the rest.
